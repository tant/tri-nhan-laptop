# Trí Nhân Laptop: Multi-Business Platform Analysis

**Project Scope Discovery:** From repair shop management system to enterprise multi-business platform

## 🎯 Key Discovery

**Trí Nhân Laptop operates a sophisticated 5-business ecosystem:**

1. **🔧 Repair Services** - 6-step workflow, 3 service tiers, geographic coverage
2. **🛒 Device Trading** - Used laptop/MacBook marketplace
3. **📦 Parts Distribution** - B2B component supply network
4. **🎓 Training Academy** - Professional courses (400K-2M VND) via Zoom
5. **🤝 Repair Outsourcing** - Partners collect devices, send to hub, return to customers

## 📊 Business Model Analysis

### Revenue Streams
- **Repair Services**: Instant (+30%), Standard, Urgent (+30%) tiers
- **Training Academy**: 400,000 - 2,000,000 VND per course module
- **Parts Distribution**: B2B wholesale with tiered pricing
- **Repair Outsourcing**: Commission from partners who collect/return devices
- **Marketplace**: Used device sales with repair integration

### Geographic Strategy
- **Hub**: HCM City (251 Vườn Lài, Tân Phú) - all repairs done here
- **Expansion**: Simple outsourcing partners collect/return devices
- **Territory Control**: 1 partner per county, maximum 3 per province
- **Service Coverage**: 5km pickup radius from hub + partner collection points

## 🔄 Current State

### What They Do Well
- ✅ **Proven Business Model**: Multiple validated revenue streams
- ✅ **Professional Processes**: 6-step repair workflow, 3-part documentation
- ✅ **Market Position**: Established training academy and simple outsourcing model
- ✅ **Quality Standards**: Component verification, testing protocols
- ✅ **Revenue Diversification**: 5 business lines reduce risk

### Critical Gaps
- ❌ **Fragmented Systems**: Manual coordination across business lines
- ❌ **Paper-Based Processes**: 3-part tickets, manual documentation
- ❌ **Limited Automation**: Phone/email coordination for all services
- ❌ **Siloed Data**: No cross-business analytics or integration
- ❌ **Manual Partner Coordination**: Phone-based device collection/return tracking

```
🔄 Integrated workflows (repair → training → parts → outsourcing)
⚡ Real-time automation replacing manual processes
🤝 Unified customer profiles with cross-business history
```

- **Integrated Analytics**: Complete customer lifetime value optimization
