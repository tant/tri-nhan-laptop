# Story 3.2.3: Cost Tracking & Billing Integration

## Status
Ready for Review

## Story
**As a** repair shop owner,
**I want** comprehensive cost tracking that automatically calculates total repair costs including parts and labor with profit margin analysis,
**so that** I can ensure profitable operations and provide accurate quotes to customers.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Automatic Cost Calculation**: Total repair cost automatically calculated from parts + labor costs
2. **Vietnamese Currency Formatting**: All costs displayed in Vietnamese Dong (VND) with proper formatting
3. **Profit Margin Analysis**: Calculate and display profit margins per repair with parts vs labor breakdown
4. **Quote Generation**: Generate customer quotes with itemized parts and labor costs
5. **Cost History**: Track cost changes throughout repair lifecycle with change reasons
6. **Billing Integration**: Seamless integration with existing repair ticket billing system
7. **Analytics Feed**: Parts cost data feeds into business analytics for insights
8. **Multi-currency Support**: Handle supplier costs in different currencies with VND conversion
9. **Real-time Updates**: Cost calculations update immediately when parts or labor estimates change
10. **Export Capability**: Export cost reports for accounting and business analysis
