# Story 3.1.2: Comprehensive Stock Management Interface

## Status
Ready for Review

## Story
**As a** repair shop staff member,
**I want** to monitor stock levels with visual indicators and receive automated alerts for low inventory,
**so that** I can maintain adequate parts inventory and avoid repair delays due to stock-outs.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Visual Stock Indicators**: Color-coded stock level displays (green: adequate, yellow: low, red: out of stock)
2. **Low Stock Alerts**: Automated notifications when parts fall below minimum stock levels
3. **Stock Adjustment Interface**: Easy-to-use interface for adjusting stock levels with reason codes
4. **Inventory Dashboard**: Overview showing total inventory value, low stock items, and recent movements
5. **Real-time Updates**: Stock levels update immediately when changes occur
6. **Vietnamese Formatting**: All stock numbers and currency values use Vietnamese formatting (VND)
7. **Permission Controls**: Only authorized staff can adjust stock levels
8. **Integration Requirement**: Works seamlessly with existing notification system
9. **Audit Trail**: All stock adjustments logged with timestamp and user information
10. **Mobile Support**: Stock monitoring accessible on mobile devices for warehouse work
