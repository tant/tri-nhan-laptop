# Story 3.2.1: Parts Selection in Repair Workflow

## Status
Ready for Review

## Story
**As a** repair technician,
**I want** to select and add parts to repair tickets directly within the repair workflow with real-time availability checking,
**so that** I can create accurate repair quotes and ensure parts are available before committing to repairs.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Integrated Parts Picker**: Parts selection component embedded in repair ticket interface
2. **Real-time Availability**: Live stock checking prevents selection of unavailable parts
3. **Smart Search**: Find parts by name, compatibility with customer's laptop model, or category
4. **Automatic Cost Calculation**: Repair quote updates automatically when parts are added/removed
5. **Parts Reservation**: Selected parts temporarily reserved during repair planning phase
6. **Vietnamese Interface**: All parts selection interface elements in Vietnamese
7. **Workflow Integration**: Parts selection available at appropriate repair workflow stages
8. **Validation**: Prevent proceeding if required parts are unavailable
9. **Mobile Support**: Parts selection works on tablets used in repair area
10. **Performance**: Parts picker loads within 2 seconds and search responds within 1 second
