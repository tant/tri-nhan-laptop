# Story 2.1.2: Customer Profile Management System

## Status
Approved

## Story
**As a** repair shop staff member,
**I want** comprehensive customer profile management with Vietnamese personal information and contact details,
**so that** I can maintain accurate customer records and provide personalized service throughout the repair process.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Vietnamese Personal Data**: Support for Vietnamese names, addresses, and contact information with proper validation
2. **Address Management**: Vietnamese address format with province, district, ward structure
3. **Multiple Contact Methods**: Phone, email, and alternative contact information management
4. **Customer Notes**: Staff notes and customer preferences tracking for personalized service
5. **Customer Categories**: Business vs individual customers with appropriate information fields
6. **Data Validation**: Comprehensive validation for Vietnamese names, addresses, and contact formats
7. **Customer Search**: Advanced search by name, phone, address, or other customer attributes
8. **Profile Updates**: Easy customer information updates with change tracking and history
9. **Privacy Controls**: Customer data privacy settings and consent management
