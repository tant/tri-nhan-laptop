# Story 2.1.3: Customer History & Repair Tracking System

## Status
Completed

## Story
**As a** repair shop staff member,
**I want** comprehensive customer repair history tracking with detailed service records,
**so that** I can provide informed service based on previous repairs and maintain long-term customer relationships.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Complete Repair History**: Chronological display of all customer repairs with status, dates, and outcomes
2. **Device Tracking**: Track multiple devices per customer with repair history for each device
3. **Service Pattern Analysis**: Identify recurring issues and common repair patterns for customers
4. **Vietnamese Service Notes**: Detailed service notes in Vietnamese with technician attributions
5. **Warranty Tracking**: Track warranty periods and warranty-covered repairs with expiration dates
6. **Cost History**: Historical repair costs and payment tracking for customer financial analysis
7. **Customer Preferences**: Remember customer preferences for communication, service, and repair approaches
8. **Quick Access Dashboard**: Customer summary dashboard with key metrics and recent activity
9. **Export Customer History**: Generate customer history reports for service analysis and documentation
10. **Integration with Current Repairs**: Seamless connection between historical data and active repair tickets
