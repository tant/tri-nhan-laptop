# Epic 3.2: Inventory Integration System

## Status: ✅ **COMPLETE**
**Priority**: Medium | **Effort**: 2-3 weeks | **Dependencies**: Epic 3.1, 2.2

---

## 🎯 **Epic Overview**

**As a** Vietnamese repair shop,
**We need** integrated inventory management with repair workflow and billing systems,
**So that** we can automatically track parts usage, update inventory, and calculate accurate repair costs.

### **Business Value**
- **Automated Inventory**: Parts automatically deducted from inventory during repairs
- **Accurate Costing**: Real-time parts cost calculation for repair estimates
- **Billing Integration**: Seamless parts cost integration with repair billing
- **Inventory Intelligence**: Analytics and reporting for inventory optimization
