## Status: ✅ **COMPLETE**
**Priority**: Medium | **Effort**: 2-3 weeks | **Dependencies**: Epic 1.x, 2.x

## 🎯 **Epic Overview**

**As a** Vietnamese repair shop,
**We need** a comprehensive parts catalog and inventory management system,
**So that** we can track laptop parts, compatibility, stock levels, and integrate parts usage with repair workflows.

### **Business Value**
- **Complete Parts Catalog**: Comprehensive laptop parts database with compatibility tracking
- **Inventory Management**: Real-time stock level monitoring and low stock alerts
- **Repair Integration**: Seamless parts usage tracking in repair workflows
- **Cost Control**: Parts cost tracking and billing integration
