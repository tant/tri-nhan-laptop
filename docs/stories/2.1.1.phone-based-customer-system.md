# Story 2.1.1: Phone-Based Customer Identification System

## Status
Approved

## Story
**As a** repair shop staff member,
**I want** a customer management system that uses phone numbers as primary identification following Vietnamese business practices,
**so that** I can quickly identify returning customers and maintain accurate customer records efficiently.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Phone Number Primary Key**: Phone numbers serve as unique customer identifiers with proper Vietnamese formatting
2. **Automatic Customer Creation**: New customer records automatically created when first repair ticket is submitted
3. **Phone Number Validation**: Vietnamese phone number format validation with support for mobile and landline formats
4. **Duplicate Prevention**: System prevents duplicate customer creation with phone number conflict detection
5. **Customer Search**: Fast customer lookup by phone number with partial matching and format flexibility
6. **Vietnamese Phone Formatting**: Display phone numbers in standard Vietnamese format (e.g., 0901 234 567)
7. **International Support**: Handle international phone numbers for foreign customers when needed
8. **Phone Number History**: Track phone number changes and maintain contact history
9. **Privacy Protection**: Secure phone number storage with appropriate access controls
10. **Integration Ready**: Phone-based system integrates seamlessly with repair ticket creation workflow
