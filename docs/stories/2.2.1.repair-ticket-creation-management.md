# Story 2.2.1: Repair Ticket Creation & Management System

## Status
Approved

## Story
**As a** repair shop staff member,
**I want** comprehensive repair ticket creation and management with Vietnamese device information and customer integration,
**so that** I can efficiently process repair requests and maintain detailed service records throughout the repair lifecycle.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Ticket Creation Workflow**: Streamlined repair ticket creation with customer selection and device information entry
2. **Vietnamese Device Data**: Support for Vietnamese device brands, models, and technical specifications
3. **Problem Documentation**: Detailed problem description in Vietnamese with photo attachment support
4. **Customer Integration**: Seamless integration with phone-based customer identification system
5. **Initial Assessment**: Preliminary diagnosis and repair estimation with Vietnamese technical notes
6. **Device Condition Recording**: Initial device condition documentation with visual damage assessment
7. **Repair Priority System**: Priority assignment (low, normal, high, urgent) with Vietnamese labels
8. **Staff Assignment**: Technician assignment and workload distribution management
9. **Vietnamese Labels**: All form fields, statuses, and interface elements in Vietnamese
10. **Draft & Template System**: Save incomplete tickets as drafts and create templates for common repairs
